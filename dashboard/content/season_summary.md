The 2025 ATP Tour season was completely dominated by Carlos Alcaraz and Jannik Sinner. They split all four Grand Slam titles evenly, with Sinner capturing the Australian Open and Wimbledon, while Alcaraz claimed a five-set thriller at the French Open and the US Open.

The battle for the world ranking lasted until the Nitto ATP Finals in Turin, where Carlos Alcaraz clinched the Year-End No. 1 spot with eight titles overall. However, Sinner finished the year on a high note by defeating Alcaraz in the Turin final to defend his title.

Behind the top two, veteran Novak Djokovic reached the milestone of 101 career ATP titles with a victory in Athens. Meanwhile, a new generation stepped into the spotlight as rising stars like João Fonseca and Learner Tien captured their first tour-level titles.
